About SIZO

Learn more about who we are, what we do and our commitment to supporting stronger communities.

Who We Are

SIZO Community Restoration Projects is focused on supporting communities through restoration, development and community-focused initiatives.

We aim to encourage people to work together, participate in community projects and contribute towards improving the spaces in which they live.

Through community involvement and practical initiatives, SIZO seeks to create opportunities for positive and sustainable change.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support meaningful community restoration and development projects that encourage participation, cooperation and sustainable improvement.

We believe that community members can play an important role in identifying local needs and contributing to solutions that benefit everyone.

Our Vision

Our vision is to contribute towards communities where people have opportunities to participate, work together and help create safe, welcoming and sustainable spaces.

Our Goals

  • Support community development initiatives.
  • Encourage community participation and involvement.
  • Assist with the restoration of community spaces.
  • Promote cooperation between community members.
  • Encourage sustainable community improvement.
  • Create awareness of opportunities to contribute to community projects.

Our Values

Community

We value cooperation and encourage people to work together towards shared goals.

Participation

We believe that community members should have opportunities to participate in initiatives that affect their surroundings.

Sustainability

We support approaches that can contribute to positive and lasting improvements.
