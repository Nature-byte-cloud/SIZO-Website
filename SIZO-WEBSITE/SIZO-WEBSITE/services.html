What We Offer

Community Development

We support initiatives that focus on improving communities and encouraging people to participate in local development.

Our approach encourages cooperation and community involvement when identifying and addressing local needs.

Community Restoration

Restoration projects focus on improving shared community spaces and creating environments that are useful and welcoming.

These initiatives can help communities take pride in their surroundings and encourage continued participation.

Community Support

SIZO encourages individuals and organisations to contribute to community-focused initiatives.

Support can include participation, volunteering, sharing ideas or helping to promote community projects.

Our Approach

SIZO focuses on community participation and practical approaches to restoration and development. We believe that successful community projects should consider the needs of the people who use and benefit from them.

We aim to encourage collaboration between community members and other people or organisations interested in supporting positive change.
